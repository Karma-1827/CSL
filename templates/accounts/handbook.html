一、使用前先了解 Before you start

1.1 適用對象 Who this guide is for

本手冊中的「老師」是指系統畫面顯示為「老師 / Teacher」的使用者，包括：

In this guide, "teacher" refers to any account the system displays as "老師 / Teacher," including:

  • 參與師大外籍生華語輔導計畫的華語輔導老師。
    Chinese tutors in the NTNU international-student tutoring program.
  • 參與馬里蘭大學合作計畫的華語老師。
    Chinese teachers in the University of Maryland partner program.
  • 由系辦安排至其他合作計畫的華語老師。
    Teachers the department office assigns to other partner programs.

各計畫可使用的媒合、文件及證明功能不完全相同。畫面沒有出現某個選單時，通常代表該帳號所屬計畫不使用該功能，不一定是系統故障。

Matching, document, and certificate features differ by program. If a menu doesn't appear, it usually means your program doesn't use that feature — not that something is broken.

1.2 開始前請準備 Before you begin

  • 系辦提供或確認過的學號。
    A student ID provided or confirmed by the department office.
  • 名冊上的身分別，例如本地生、僑生、港澳生、陸生、外籍生或馬里蘭學生。
    Your identity category on the roster, e.g. local, overseas Chinese, Hong Kong/Macao, mainland Chinese, international, or Maryland student.
  • 可正常收信的 Email。
    A working email address.
  • 口語能力證明檔案，格式依上傳頁說明辦理。
    An oral proficiency document, in the format described on the upload page.
  • 自己能記住的三組安全問題與答案。
    Three security questions and answers you'll remember.

學號與身分別必須和系辦匯入的名冊一致，否則無法註冊。英文字母大小寫不影響學號辨識，但仍建議依正式學號格式輸入。

Your student ID and identity category must match the department's roster, or registration will fail. Letter case doesn't affect ID matching, but entering it in the official format is still recommended.

1.3 個資與帳號安全 Privacy & account security

  • 不要共用帳號或密碼。
    Never share your account or password.
  • 不要把密碼、安全問題答案或未公開的學生資料傳給他人。
    Never send your password, security answers, or private student information to anyone else.
  • 使用公用電腦後務必登出並關閉瀏覽器。
    Always sign out and close the browser after using a shared computer.
  • 課堂佐證連結只開放給配對對象與管理人員查看，不要設為公開搜尋。
    Class evidence links should only be viewable by your matched partner and administrators — never make them publicly searchable.

二、第一次註冊 First-time registration

2.1 開啟註冊頁 Open the registration page

  1. 開啟正式系統首頁。
    Open the production site's homepage.
  2. 按「我要註冊 / Register」。
    Click "我要註冊 / Register."
  3. 選擇「老師 / Teacher」。
    Choose "老師 / Teacher."

2.2 建立帳號 Create your account

  1. 輸入學號。
    Enter your student ID.
  2. 選擇與名冊相同的身分別。
    Select the identity category matching the roster.
  3. 設定至少 10 個字元的密碼。
    Set a password at least 10 characters long.
  4. 再輸入一次相同密碼。
    Enter the same password again.
  5. 按「註冊並填寫個人資料 / Register and continue」。
    Click "註冊並填寫個人資料 / Register and continue."

如果系統顯示資料不符，先檢查學號及身分別。仍無法註冊時，請將學號、選擇的身分別及錯誤畫面交給管理員確認名冊，不要反覆嘗試不同身分別。

If the system reports a mismatch, double-check your student ID and identity category first. If registration still fails, give your student ID, the identity category you selected, and a screenshot of the error to an administrator to check the roster — don't keep guessing different categories.

2.3 確認名冊資料 Confirm your roster record

系統會顯示學號與角色。確認資料正確後繼續填寫；若角色不是「老師 / Teacher」，請停止註冊並聯絡管理員。

The system displays your student ID and role. Continue only after confirming they're correct; if the role isn't "老師 / Teacher," stop and contact an administrator.

2.4 填寫個人與教學資料 Complete your profile & teaching information

依畫面欄位填寫或選擇資料。必填欄位未完成時，系統會在欄位附近顯示提示。

Fill in or select each field as prompted. If a required field is missing, the system shows a message near that field.

填寫時請注意：

Please note while filling this in:

  • 姓名請使用正式資料，不要填暱稱。
    Use your official name, not a nickname.
  • Email 請填可正常收信的信箱。
    Enter an email address you actually check.
  • 教學能力評估請依自己的實際情況選擇。
    Rate your teaching abilities honestly.
  • 可上課時間會顯示在匿名媒合資料中，請填寫真正能配合的時段。
    Your availability appears in anonymous matching data, so list only times you can actually keep.

2.5 設定安全問題 Set your security questions

  1. 選擇三個不同的安全問題；已選過的問題不能重複選擇。
    Choose three different security questions; a question already chosen can't be selected again.
  2. 每題填寫至少 3 個字元的答案。
    Enter an answer at least 3 characters long for each.
  3. 記住答案的文字、空格與寫法，忘記密碼時會用來驗證身分。
    Remember the exact wording, spacing, and spelling — these answers verify your identity if you forget your password.

安全問題答案不應使用密碼，也不要選擇別人容易從社群網站猜到的內容。

Don't reuse your password as a security answer, and avoid answers others could easily guess from your social media.

2.6 上傳口語能力證明 Upload your oral proficiency document

依畫面說明選擇檔案並上傳。完成註冊後，文件會進入管理員審核流程；審核通過前仍可登入及查看資料，但不能開始尋找學生。

Choose and upload a file as instructed on screen. After registration, it enters the administrator review queue; you can still sign in and view your data before approval, but can't start browsing students yet.

2.7 完成註冊 Finish registration

確認個人資料、教學資料、安全問題、口語能力證明及可上課時間後，勾選同意事項並送出。註冊完成後會直接進入老師使用介面。

After reviewing your profile, teaching information, security questions, oral proficiency document, and availability, check the consent box and submit. You'll land directly in the teacher interface once registration is complete.

三、登入、登出與忘記密碼 Sign in, sign out & password reset

3.1 登入 Sign in

  1. 開啟正式系統首頁。
    Open the production site's homepage.
  2. 輸入學號及密碼。
    Enter your student ID and password.
  3. 按「登入 / Sign in」。
    Click "登入 / Sign in."

若登入失敗，先確認鍵盤大小寫及密碼。連續輸入錯誤時，系統可能暫時限制登入，請稍後再試，不要持續重複送出。

If sign-in fails, check Caps Lock and your password first. Repeated failed attempts may trigger a temporary lockout — wait and try again rather than resubmitting repeatedly.

3.2 登出 Sign out

按右上角「登出 / Sign out」。確認回到登入頁後再關閉瀏覽器。

Click "登出 / Sign out" in the top right. Confirm you're back on the sign-in page before closing the browser.

3.3 忘記密碼 Forgot password

  1. 在登入頁按「忘記密碼 / Forgot password」。
    On the sign-in page, click "忘記密碼 / Forgot password."
  2. 輸入學號並繼續。
    Enter your student ID and continue.
  3. 回答註冊時設定的三個安全問題。
    Answer the three security questions you set during registration.
  4. 驗證成功後設定新密碼，並再次輸入確認。
    Once verified, set a new password and enter it again to confirm.
  5. 回到登入頁，以新密碼登入。
    Return to the sign-in page and sign in with the new password.

系統不會顯示原密碼。若忘記安全問題答案，請聯絡管理員處理，不要將可能的答案交給他人代試。

The system never shows your original password. If you've forgotten your security answers, contact an administrator — don't hand your possible answers to someone else to try.

六、尋找學生與邀請配對 Finding students & invitations

6.1 開放條件 Eligibility conditions

老師須同時符合以下條件，才會看到可邀請的學生：

A teacher must meet all of the following to see invitable students:

  • 已完成註冊及個人資料。
    Registration and profile are complete.
  • 口語能力證明已通過。
    The oral proficiency document is approved.
  • 目前計畫及學期已開放媒合。
    The current program and semester are open for matching.
  • 尚有配對名額。
    You still have an open matching slot.

若畫面沒有學生，可能是目前沒有符合條件且尚未配對的學生，不代表系統一定有問題。

If no students appear, there may simply be no eligible, unmatched students right now — it doesn't necessarily mean something is wrong.

6.2 查看匿名學生資料 Browse anonymous student profiles

按「尋找學生 / Find students」。配對成立前，系統只顯示學習需求、程度、語言、可上課時間等媒合資料，不顯示姓名、學號及 Email。

Click "尋找學生 / Find students." Before a match is made, the system shows only matching data — learning needs, level, language, and availability — never a name, student ID, or email.

老師只能看到自己可服務計畫中的學生：

A teacher only sees students in the program(s) they're eligible to serve:

  • 一般華語輔導老師看到師大外籍生計畫的學生。
    General Chinese tutors see NTNU international-student program students.
  • 馬里蘭計畫老師看到馬里蘭計畫學生。
    Maryland program teachers see Maryland program students.
  • 其他合作計畫依名冊與系統設定顯示。
    Other partner programs follow the roster and system configuration.

可按「搜尋條件 / Search filters」縮小結果，例如華語程度、加強項目、性別、母語、日期及時段。

Use "搜尋條件 / Search filters" to narrow results by Chinese level, target skills, gender, native language, weekday, and time slot.

6.3 送出邀請 Send an invitation

  1. 閱讀學生匿名資料。
    Review the student's anonymous profile.
  2. 確認學習需求與時間大致可以配合。
    Confirm the learning needs and schedule are broadly compatible.
  3. 按「邀請學生 / Invite student」。
    Click "邀請學生 / Invite student."
  4. 到「邀請管理」查看等待回覆狀態。
    Check "邀請管理 / Invitations" for the pending status.

邀請有效期限為 5 天。每位使用者同一學期最多同時有 3 筆待回覆邀請；同一組已有待回覆邀請時，不能重複送出。

Invitations are valid for 5 days. Each user may have at most 3 pending invitations per semester, and you can't send a second invitation to the same person while one is already pending.

6.4 取消尚未回覆的邀請 Cancel a pending invitation

發起邀請的人可在「邀請管理」取消尚未回覆的邀請。取消後若仍有媒合名額，可重新選擇其他對象。

Whoever sent the invitation can cancel it from "邀請管理 / Invitations" before it's answered. If you still have an open slot afterward, you may invite someone else.

6.5 收到學生邀請 Receiving a student's invitation

只有所屬計畫允許學生主動邀請時，老師才會收到學生送出的邀請；目前主要適用於馬里蘭計畫。

Teachers only receive student-initiated invitations if their program allows it — currently mainly the Maryland program.

  1. 按「邀請管理」。
    Click "邀請管理 / Invitations."
  2. 查看收到的邀請與匿名資料。
    Review the received invitation and anonymous profile.
  3. 選擇接受或婉拒。
    Choose to accept or decline.

接受後配對立即成立，雙方才會看到姓名、學號及 Email。一般自行媒合流程中，一位老師同一學期最多有 2 位進行中的學生；名額已滿時，系統不再接受新的自行媒合邀請。

Accepting creates the match immediately, and only then do both sides see names, student IDs, and emails. Under the ordinary self-matching flow, a teacher may have up to 2 active students per semester; once full, the system stops accepting new self-matched invitations.

七、配對成立後 After a match is made

7.1 查看學生資料 View your student's profile

回到「我的首頁」，在「目前配對」查看學生。點「查看資料 / View profile」可查看配對後開放的基本資料。

Go back to "我的首頁 / Overview" and find your student under "目前配對 / Current matches." Click "查看資料 / View profile" to see the details unlocked after matching.

7.2 使用私訊 Using messages

  1. 按左側「私訊 / Messages」，或在配對卡片按「私訊 / Message」。
    Click "私訊 / Messages" in the sidebar, or "私訊 / Message" on the match card.
  2. 選擇學生。
    Select the student.
  3. 輸入訊息並送出。
    Type your message and send it.

私訊適合確認上課時間、地點與一般課務事項。緊急事件或安全問題不要只留在私訊中，應另外聯絡管理員。

Messages are meant for confirming class times, locations, and routine matters. For emergencies or safety concerns, always contact an administrator directly instead of relying on messages alone.

7.3 申請解除配對 Requesting a pairing release

在配對卡片找到「解除配對 / Release pairing」，選擇原因並送出。

Find "解除配對 / Release pairing" on the match card, choose a reason, and submit.

  • 未出席、失去聯絡或時間無法配合：管理員 48 小時內未處理時，系統會自動解除。
    No-show, unreachable, or scheduling conflict: the system auto-releases the pairing if an administrator hasn't acted within 48 hours.
  • 態度或行為問題、其他原因：必須填寫說明，並等待管理員審核。
    Conduct issues or other reasons: you must provide an explanation and wait for administrator review.

申請送出後，在系統顯示配對已解除前，請不要自行假設配對已經終止。解除後，已建立的歷史課程與訊息仍會保留供查閱。

After submitting, don't assume the pairing has ended until the system confirms it. Once released, existing class history and messages remain viewable.

8.2 建立每週重複課程 Repeat weekly

建立課程時勾選「每週重複 / Repeat weekly」，再選擇重複結束日。系統會逐週建立課程，直到指定日期或學期結束日；只要其中一堂超過時數上限，整次建立就不會完成。

When creating a class, check "每週重複 / Repeat weekly" and pick an end date. The system creates one class per week up to that date or the semester's end, whichever comes first; if any single occurrence would exceed an hour quota, the whole batch fails to create.

8.3 修改課程 Reschedule a class

  1. 在課表點「查看課程 / View class」（或直接點課程旁的「修改」標籤）。
    On the schedule, click "查看課程 / View class" (or click the "修改 / Reschedule" tag next to the class directly).
  2. 往下找到修改區塊。
    Scroll down to the reschedule section.
  3. 選擇新日期、時間及時數。
    Choose the new date, time, and duration.
  4. 重複課程可選擇只修改本堂，或修改本堂及之後的課程。
    For a repeating class, choose to edit just this occurrence, or this one and all future occurrences.
  5. 儲存後回到課表確認變更。
    Save, then return to the schedule to confirm the change.

修改後的課程必須在未來、學期期間內，且仍符合每週與每學期時數限制。課程已有簽到或紀錄時不能自行修改；課程結束超過 3 週也不能修改，需聯絡管理員確認。

The rescheduled class must still be in the future, within the semester, and within the weekly/semester hour limits. A class with any check-in or record can no longer be self-edited; a class more than 3 weeks past its end also can't be edited — contact an administrator instead.

8.4 取消課程 Cancel a class

  1. 在課表開啟該堂課（或直接點課程旁的「取消」標籤）。
    Open the class from the schedule (or click the "取消 / Cancel" tag next to it directly).
  2. 找到取消課程區塊。
    Find the cancel-class section.
  3. 填寫取消原因並送出。
    Enter a cancellation reason and submit.
  4. 回到課表確認課程狀態。
    Return to the schedule to confirm the status.

一堂課必須同時符合下列條件，才會列入有效時數：

A class only counts as verified hours once all of the following are true:

  1. 老師與學生都完成簽到。

  2. 老師與學生都提交課堂紀錄。
