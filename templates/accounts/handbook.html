USER GUIDE · V3.1

使用手冊 User guide

依照您的使用身分，整理目前系統可使用的主要功能。
A role-based guide to the features currently available in the system.

{{ request.user.get_role_display }}
{% if request.user.role == 'TUTOR' %}
01

個人資料與口語能力 Profile & oral proficiency

確認個人資料、教學履歷、上課時間與口語能力證明的審核狀態；口語能力審核通過後才能配對。

Review your profile, teaching information, availability, and oral proficiency status. Matching opens once your oral proficiency is approved.

02

尋找學生 Find students

瀏覽配對所需的匿名學習資料，並向合適的學生發出邀請；配對前不會顯示姓名、學號或聯絡方式。

Browse anonymous learning profiles and invite a suitable student. Identity details stay hidden until a match is made.

03

篩選候選學生 Filter students

可用性別、華語程度、母語、加強項目、星期與時段縮小候選名單；點擊「搜尋條件」展開篩選面板。

Narrow the candidate list by gender, Chinese level, native language, target skills, weekday, and time slot via the collapsible search filter panel.

04

邀請與配對 Invitations & matching

邀請五天內有效；雙方同意後即建立配對。每學期可同時輔導兩位學生，待回覆邀請上限 3 筆。

Invitations are valid for five days. A match begins after both sides agree; up to two students may be active at once, with a cap of three pending invitations.

05

排課 Scheduling classes

只有老師可以排課、修改或取消；每堂 0.5–2 小時，分鐘須為 5 的倍數，可設定每週重複。每組配對每週上限 2 小時、每學期上限 32 小時，個人每學期上限 64 小時。

Only teachers can create, edit, or cancel classes. Each session is 0.5–2 hours in 5-minute increments and may repeat weekly, subject to weekly/semester hour quotas.

06

簽到與課堂紀錄 Check-in & class records

上課前 10 分鐘開放簽到；雙方各自填寫課堂紀錄，可標記本次練習的聽說讀寫項目。

  1. 提供 1–5 個當次上課佐證連結，例如上課畫面截圖、教材、作業或錄影。
  2. 確認分享權限（共用檢視權限），讓對方及管理者可直接查看。
  3. 實習／輔導階段結束後至少 10 天再刪除或下架；查核時無法查看，該堂時數可能不予採計。

Both participants submit their own class record and 1–5 evidence links. Make sure the links can be viewed by your partner and administrators, and keep them available for at least 10 days after the practicum or tutoring stage ends.

07

雙方確認與補登 Mutual confirmation & makeup

需確認對方的簽到與紀錄才算完成；逾時簽到或補寫紀錄須填寫原因，且需經 Admin 核准才計入有效時數。

Both sides must confirm each other's check-in and record. Late check-ins or records require a reason and admin approval before counting as verified hours.

08

私訊 Messages

配對成立後可與學生私訊；「私訊」頁籤會顯示未讀數、最後一則訊息與時間，依最近活動排序。解除配對後仍可查看歷史對話。

Message your matched student directly. The messages tab shows unread counts, last message previews, and timestamps, sorted by recent activity; past conversations remain viewable after a release.

09

課堂通報與異常回報 Class alerts & incident reports

上課時間內可送出即時通報（如聯絡不到對方）；任何時候都可對已上過的課提出異常回報（如場地、進度或人身安全問題）供 Admin 留存紀錄。

Send an urgent alert during class time (e.g. unreachable partner), or file an incident report anytime after a class for issues like venue, progress, or safety concerns.

10

解除配對 Pairing release

可從目前配對提出解除申請；部分原因 48 小時未處理會自動解除，行為或其他問題則由管理員審核。

Request a release from the current match. Eligible reasons auto-release after 48 hours; conduct and other concerns require review.

11

時數與證明下載 Hours & certificates

本學期結束後第 3 天開放下載本學期證明；已結束的學期隨時可下載。可選擇整學期或自訂日期範圍，並選擇摘要版或詳細版 PDF。

This semester's certificate becomes downloadable on day 3 after it ends; past semesters are always available. Choose the date range and a summary or detailed PDF.

{% elif request.user.role == 'TUTEE' %}
01

個人與學習資料 Profile & learning needs

確認基本資料、華語程度、希望加強項目與可上課時間。

Review your basic details, Chinese level, target skills, and availability.

02

收到邀請 Received invitations

在邀請到期前查看匿名老師資料，選擇接受或婉拒。

Review the anonymous teacher profile and respond before the invitation expires.

03

配對與解除 Matching & release

同一時間只能與一位老師配對；解除後可找新老師，但同學期不會再次配對到原老師。

Only one teacher may be active at a time. After release, you may find a new teacher but cannot rematch with the same teacher that semester.

{% if is_maryland %}
04

主動尋找老師 Find a teacher

合作計畫學生可瀏覽匿名老師資料並主動發出邀請；可用性別、母語、星期與時段縮小候選名單。

Partner-program students may browse anonymous teacher profiles and send invitations, narrowing the list by gender, native language, weekday, and time slot.

{% endif %}
05

課程與簽到 Classes & check-in

課程由老師安排，您會在「我的課程」看到排定的時間；上課前 10 分鐘可簽到，課堂開始後可填寫自己的課堂紀錄並選擇性上傳附件。

Your teacher schedules classes, visible under "My classes." Check in up to 10 minutes early, and submit your own class record (with an optional attachment) once class has started.

06

雙方確認與補登 Mutual confirmation & makeup

需確認老師的簽到與紀錄才算完成；逾時簽到或補寫紀錄須填寫原因，且需經 Admin 核准才計入有效時數。

You must confirm your teacher's check-in and record. Late check-ins or records require a reason and admin approval before counting as verified hours.

07

私訊 Messages

配對成立後可與老師私訊；「私訊」頁籤會顯示未讀數、最後一則訊息與時間。解除配對後仍可查看歷史對話。

Message your matched teacher directly. The messages tab shows unread counts and last message previews; past conversations remain viewable after a release.

08

課堂通報與異常回報 Class alerts & incident reports

上課時間內可送出即時通報（如聯絡不到老師）；任何時候都可對已上過的課提出異常回報供 Admin 留存紀錄。

Send an urgent alert during class time (e.g. unreachable teacher), or file an incident report anytime after a class for Admin to record.

09

時數與證明下載 Hours & certificates

若所屬合作計畫開放下載，本學期結束後第 3 天可下載本學期證明；已結束的學期隨時可下載，格式與老師相同。

If your program allows hour downloads, this semester's certificate becomes available on day 3 after it ends; past semesters are always downloadable, in the same format as teachers.

{% else %}
01

名冊匯入 Roster import

在管理首頁「名冊匯入」頁籤，依角色與合作計畫分卡片上傳「學號＋身分別」名單；舊的單欄學號清單仍可使用。重新匯入可補上既有空白身分別，但不會覆蓋已存在的資料。

Upload a student ID and identity-category list under the matching role/program card. Legacy ID-only files remain supported. Re-importing may fill blank identities without overwriting existing data.

02

口語能力審核 Oral proficiency review

審核老師上傳的口語能力證明；審核通過後老師才能瀏覽學生資料並發出邀請。

Review teachers' uploaded oral proficiency documents. Approval is required before a teacher can browse students or send invitations.

03

學期設定 Semester settings

新增、編輯或封存學期；可依合作計畫分別設定期間，數量不設上限，但同一計畫的啟用期間不可重疊。已有配對紀錄的學期只能封存，不能刪除。

Create, edit, or archive semesters, scoped per partner program with no limit on how many exist; enabled periods for the same program cannot overlap. Semesters with existing pairings can only be archived, not deleted.

04

配對與解除審核 Matching & release review

查看配對總覽與解除配對申請；部分原因需要 Admin 核准或拒絕，其餘原因逾 48 小時自動解除。

Review matching status and pairing-release requests. Some reasons need explicit approval; others auto-release after 48 hours.

05

課程總覽與行政檔案 Class overview & user profiles

查看全系課程、老師課表，並可從學生名冊點「查看檔案」開啟單一使用者的唯讀彙整頁（基本資料、配對、課程時數、通報紀錄）。

Browse system-wide classes and teacher schedules, or open a read-only aggregated profile for any user (basic info, pairings, hours, reports) from the roster's "view profile" link.

06

課堂通報與異常回報 Class alerts & incident reports

查看待處理與已紀錄的通報／回報，可標記為「已紀錄」並留備註；「已紀錄」代表已知悉留存，不代表問題已解決。

Review pending and recorded alerts/reports, and mark them "recorded" with a note — recorded means acknowledged and kept on file, not necessarily resolved.

07

補簽到／補紀錄審核 Late check-in/record approval

逐筆核准或拒絕補簽到與補課堂紀錄；被拒絕的紀錄不計入有效時數。

Approve or reject late check-ins and late class records individually. Rejected entries do not count toward verified hours.

08

時數修正與匯出 Hour adjustments & exports

可在 Django Admin 新增「行政更正」紀錄補登系統紀錄以外的時數（只能加不能扣）；另可依使用者、學期或日期範圍匯出報表。

Add an administrative correction in Django Admin for hours outside the system's own records (additive only); export reports by user, semester, or date range.
