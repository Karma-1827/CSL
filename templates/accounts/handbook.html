使用手冊 User guide

依照您的使用身分，整理目前系統可使用的主要功能。
A role-based guide to the features currently available in the system.

{{ request.user.get_role_display }}
{% if request.user.role == 'TUTOR' %}
01

個人資料與資格 Profile & qualification

確認個人資料、教學履歷、上課時間與資格文件的審核狀態。

Review your profile, teaching information, availability, and qualification status.

02

尋找學生 Find students

瀏覽配對所需的匿名學習資料，並向合適的學生發出邀請。

Browse anonymous learning profiles and invite a suitable student.

03

邀請與配對 Invitations & matching

邀請五天內有效；雙方同意後即建立配對。每學期可同時輔導兩位學生。

Invitations are valid for five days. A match begins after both sides agree; up to two students may be active at once.

04

解除配對 Pairing release

可從目前配對提出解除申請；部分原因三日未處理會自動解除，行為或其他問題則由管理員審核。

Request a release from the current match. Eligible reasons auto-release after three days; conduct and other concerns require review.

{% elif request.user.role == 'TUTEE' %}
01

個人與學習資料 Profile & learning needs

確認基本資料、華語程度、希望加強項目與可上課時間。

Review your basic details, Chinese level, target skills, and availability.

02

收到邀請 Received invitations

在邀請到期前查看匿名老師資料，選擇接受或婉拒。

Review the anonymous teacher profile and respond before the invitation expires.

03

配對與解除 Matching & release

同一時間只能與一位老師配對；解除後可找新老師，但同學期不會再次配對到原老師。

Only one teacher may be active at a time. After release, you may find a new teacher but cannot rematch with the same teacher that semester.

Partner-program students may browse anonymous teacher profiles and send invitations.
